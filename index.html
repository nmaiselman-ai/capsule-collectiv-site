The idea

Pack less. Wear more.

Tell it about your trip. It builds a carry-on capsule from your own wardrobe, styled to your taste, with an outfit for every day.

Photos from Unsplash.

10pieces make 36looks
Nº 1The problem

A full wardrobe, and still nothing to wear.

Today the routine goes like this: pick one piece, search Pinterest for how others style it, then flick between the app and the wardrobe trying to rebuild the look from what you own.

Finding inspiration is easy. Turning it into an outfit from your own clothes is the hard part.

Packing makes it harder. You need many outfits from a few pieces, for weather you can't predict and occasions you can't get wrong.

Nº 2Who it's for

People with plenty of clothes and a Pinterest habit.

The stylish packerAlready saves outfit ideas to Pinterest and Instagram, and wants a carry-on that still feels like them.
The overthinkerStands in front of a full rail every morning and can't decide. Wants a stylist who knows what they own.
Nº 3What it does

Five short chapters, from trip details to a finished packing list.

  1. 01
    The Trip

    Destination, dates with a live forecast for each day, luggage size and any special occasions, like a wedding.

  2. 02
    The Wardrobe

    Tick the basics you own from a starter library, or add your own pieces with a photo. ✦ marks the ones that suit this trip.

  3. 03
    The Mood

    Optional. Upload inspiration looks or pick a style vibe, and the colours steer your outfits.

  4. 04
    The Edit

    A look card for every day and occasion, plus the packing list. Swap pieces, lock favourites, rate looks ♥ or ✕, or reshuffle.

  5. 05
    The Checklist

    Tick pieces off as they go in the case, then share or save the list. Bon voyage.

Trip packing comes first. The same engine will later answer "What do I wear tomorrow?" and "How do I style this piece?"
